## Licence rule for this repository

This is a public repository, so what goes into Git depends on the licence of each asset:

- **CC0 and CC-BY assets** can be committed. CC-BY needs credit in [CREDITS](#credits).
- **Fab Standard License assets** (most free Fab packs) are free to use in the game but may not
  be redistributed as raw files. Do not commit them. Download them from your own Fab library into
  `Content/Fab/`, which is in `.gitignore`.

Check the "License terms" line on each listing before you commit anything from it.

## Sound and people that live in the private plugin

- **Splice samples** (room tone, birds, the wall clock, toy sounds, a music box, the door, the
  hall). Splice's licence allows them inside the game, not as loose files, so they are imported
  to `/HorrorPrivate/Audio/X_*` by `Scripts/import_private_audio.py`. Without the plugin the game
  falls back to the sounds `Scripts/make_sounds.py` synthesises.
- **MetaHuman characters** (the mother first). Made through the editor's MCP server; the steps
  are in [METAHUMAN.md](METAHUMAN.md). Epic's licence allows MetaHumans in an Unreal game, not
  as published source files, so they stay in the private plugin too.
- **MetaHuman clothes and hair from Fab** (the mother's sweater, jeans and flats by Epic Games,
  her hair by Venice, the doctors' shirt by Nyanpasinka; all free, Fab Standard License). Imported from `.mhpkg` files to
  `/HorrorPrivate/MetaHumans/Wardrobe` by `Scripts/import_metahuman_packages.py` and put on her
  by `Scripts/dress_people.py`. The listings are in the private plugin's `LICENSE-NOTES.md`.
